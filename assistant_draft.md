Mental health remains a deeply stigmatized and under-resourced domain across the Middle East, where cultural, linguistic, and structural barriers significantly limit access to care. There is a critical need for scalable, culturally sensitive mental health support that respects local norms, languages, and belief systems while expanding reach beyond the limited pool of trained mental health professionals in the region.

## How Generative AI Can Help

- **Culturally adapted conversational support:** AI-powered chatbots trained on region-specific language and cultural context can provide stigma-free, accessible first-line mental health support in Arabic dialects.
- **Scalable psychoeducation:** Generative AI can produce and personalize mental health educational content tailored to local cultural values, helping normalize help-seeking behavior across diverse communities.
- **Provider assistance and training:** AI tools can support the limited mental health workforce by assisting clinicians with documentation, case formulation, and culturally informed treatment guidance.
